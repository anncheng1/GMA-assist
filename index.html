      About GMA Assist

      GMA Assist supports clinicians and trainees using Prechtl's Method on the Qualitative Assessment of General Movements (Einspieler, Prechtl, Bos, Ferrari & Cioni; Mac Keith Press, 2004). It provides a video review workspace with slow-motion and frame stepping, marking of GM sequences, exploratory motion indicators, structured scoring (GM Optimality Score and the 3–5 month Motor Optimality Score), longitudinal trajectories, and training material with a quiz and a blind practice-clip library.

      Important limitations

      • This is a decision-support and educational tool. It does not classify general movements automatically and must not be used as a stand-alone diagnostic device.
      • Clinical GMA should be performed by assessors who have completed accredited training (e.g. General Movements Trust Basic and Advanced courses). This app does not confer certification.
      • The teaching text is an original summary for revision purposes; consult the manual and accompanying case videos for the authoritative description.

      Privacy

      Everything runs in the browser. Videos, records and training results stay on the device (localStorage / IndexedDB) unless you export them. Use pseudonymous infant IDs and follow your hospital's data-protection and consent policies when storing infant videos.

      Install / offline

      Hosted on GitHub Pages, the app can be installed (“Add to Home screen” / “Install app”) and works offline after the first visit.
